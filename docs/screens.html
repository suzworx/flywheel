Factory screens

The approved design for flywheel factory (issue #583): a k9s-style terminal UI for a person inspecting the factory by hand and working out what happened and why. Agents keep using the CLI and --json. Most of it is on main now: the layout, navigation and search, the unit's why and timeline, the pulse and metrics dashboards with their drill-downs, the frozen factory, colours and skins, and the actions on a unit. Every screen is drawn at a 110-column terminal with example data; a shipped screen shows the keys and columns the code has (The factory view describes each).

The badge on each screen says what exists today: shipped on main, building in a unit now, planned in the design only.

  • The WHY column is one plain sentence per unit, built from the same facts recover uses, so it is deterministic; the frame cuts it, the unit's detail has it whole.
  • Colours (dark skin): cyan running, green passed or done, yellow waiting, planned or needs correction, red failed or stalled, magenta suspended or frozen, dim landed. factory.skin picks dark, light or none.

Search

:s <text>shipped

Hunting a cause: one case-insensitive search across the ledger, the run logs, the reports and the briefs, one row per matching line with about 40 characters each side. Enter on an event opens the unit's log at the match; on any other result, the unit's explain tab. It stops at 500 results.

Frozen factory

shipped

When every model's tokens run out, the factory freezes itself (reason tokens-exhausted) and thaws when they return, resuming each stopped unit in its own session. flywheel suspend and flywheel resume do the same by hand (see Freeze and resume). The header says FROZEN since and until when, one line per paused model, and each suspended unit says why.

Fleet

:ctxshipped

Every factory on the machine, like k9s contexts. This screen is in flywheel factory now: :ctx lists every factory in the fleet, Enter switches the whole view to it, and --ctx <name> starts there. The fleet is also on the CLI: flywheel fleet add, list and status keep a registry of factory roots, find every ledger under them (worktrees included) and print one merged table with NAME, KIND, EVENTS, RUNNING, PASSED, FINISHED, ANDON, STATE, HEALTH and LAST (see Fleet). The fleet learnings queue and fleet watch are shipped too.
