Factory screens

The approved design for flywheel factory (issue #583): a k9s-style terminal UI for a person inspecting the factory by hand and working out what happened and why. Agents keep using the CLI and --json. Every screen is drawn at a 110-column terminal with example data.

The badge on each screen says what exists today: shipped on main, building in a unit now, planned in the design only.

Frozen factory

planned

When every model's tokens run out, the factory freezes itself and says until when. Units stopped by the freeze show why and resume in their own session at the thaw. The freeze itself is shipped (flywheel suspend and flywheel resume, see Freeze and resume); this screen and the automatic freeze are not yet.
